## 2025-05-15 - [Search Accessibility & UX]
**Learning:** Adding `aria-live="polite"` and `aria-atomic="true"` to result counts (e.g., "Showing X books") is crucial for screen reader users to understand the impact of their search/filter actions without moving focus. Decorative icons at the start of inputs and clear buttons at the end improve visual context and convenience.
**Action:** Always include ARIA live regions for dynamic result counts and provide accessible clear buttons for search inputs.
