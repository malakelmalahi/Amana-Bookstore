## 2026-05-12 - [Asynchronous Inline Feedback Consistency]
**Learning:** Inconsistent "Add to Cart" behavior across the application (inline feedback in lists vs. immediate redirect on detail pages) creates a jarring experience. Users prefer maintaining browsing context when performing non-terminal actions.
**Action:** Standardize on asynchronous inline feedback (loading states and success indicators) for all cart interactions to improve perceived performance and keep the user in their current flow.
