## 2025-05-15 - Search Feedback & Control
**Learning:** Screen reader users benefit from immediate feedback on filtered results via `aria-live`. Adding a clear search button provides a convenient shortcut for all users while maintaining focus management. Proper pluralization in result summaries adds a layer of professional polish.
**Action:** Always include `aria-live="polite"` on result counts and provide an explicit "Clear" action for text-based filters to enhance accessibility and usability.
