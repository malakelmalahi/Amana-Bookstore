## 2025-05-14 - [Consistent Async Feedback for Cart Actions]
**Learning:** Standardizing inline asynchronous feedback (loading/success states) across grid, list, and detail views improves UI predictability and prevents disruptive context switches (like redirects to the cart page) during high-intent but non-final actions.
**Action:** Use inline state indicators for "Add to Cart" to keep users in their browsing flow.
