## 2025-05-15 - [Improving Add-to-Cart Flow]
**Learning:** For e-commerce experiences, redirecting the user to the cart immediately after adding an item can be disruptive and break the browsing flow. Providing inline feedback (loading state + success confirmation) keeps users engaged on the product page, encouraging further discovery.
**Action:** Replace disruptive redirects with asynchronous inline feedback and success states on "Add to Cart" buttons.
